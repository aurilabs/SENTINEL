# Publication Roadmap

This roadmap describes possible publication units inside the SENTINEL research program. It is not a promise of publication and does not imply that novelty has been established. Each paper direction must survive literature, patent and experimental falsification before manuscript work begins.

## Paper A — Diagnostic reliability under environmental confounders

**Working objective:** quantify whether optical corrosion-reporting systems can distinguish electrochemically active corrosion from benign coating damage and environmental changes under marine-relevant conditions.

Core variables may include humidity/wetness, salinity, temperature, controlled coating defects and optical acquisition conditions. Future extensions may include UV/weathering.

Candidate metrics:

- sensitivity and specificity;
- false-positive / false-negative rate;
- response time;
- calibrated color or fluorescence change;
- uncertainty and repeatability;
- electrochemical ground truth;
- barrier-performance penalty caused by the diagnostic additive.

This is currently the highest-priority publication direction because it can generate useful evidence without first claiming a novel indicator chemistry.

## Paper B — Persistent environmental / corrosion history

**Working objective:** investigate whether a protective material can retain an interpretable record of a past critical exposure or corrosion event after the immediate trigger has disappeared.

The intended distinction is between:

- current-state reporting; and
- historical-state / cumulative-exposure reporting.

Material-memory concepts exist in adjacent mechanochromic research, so this direction requires particularly careful novelty screening.

## Paper C — Functional self-healing verification

**Working objective:** determine whether a material-integrated optical status can indicate restoration of protective function, not merely visible scratch closure.

Candidate validation channels include EIS, barrier resistance, adhesion or permeability measurements.

## Paper D — Multi-stressor state discrimination

**Working objective:** distinguish multiple degradation mechanisms while measuring cross-talk among sensing channels.

Potential stressors include mechanical damage, chloride/wetness exposure, UV/weathering and temperature cycling.

## Later application branches

### SENTINEL-Marine
Marine/offshore/subsea carbon-steel infrastructure.

### SENTINEL-GEO
Geothermal materials exposed to coupled chemical and thermal environments. Advanced testing would require an appropriate external laboratory.

### SENTINEL-H2
Hydrogen-infrastructure interfaces and degradation monitoring. Hydrogen exposure and embrittlement work must be performed in appropriately equipped laboratories.

## Publication standard

A SENTINEL manuscript should not rely on a visually impressive response alone. Claims should be supported by controls, repeatability, quantitative analysis, independent material-state measurements and an explicit discussion of failure modes and uncertainty.
